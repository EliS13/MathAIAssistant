## Question 2a
Problem: 2. Simplify completely. Use any exponent laws you need, and leave only positive exponents.
a) [(2m^3n^2)^2(4mn^3)] / (8m^2n)


### Student Work:

### Feedback
In question 2a, you squared the first set of parentheses incorrectly by forgetting to square the number 2 inside it. Remember that the exponent outside applies to every single piece on the inside, including the coefficient. Try squaring that 2 first before multiplying everything else out.

## Question 3
Problem: 3. Classify each number as a perfect square or non-perfect square. If it is a perfect square, give its principal square root.
225  30  4/9
49/20  0.16  0.6


### Student Work:

### Feedback
In question 3, your last fraction part got a bit mixed up because you only applied the square root to the top number instead of the whole fraction. Remember that when a fraction is under a square root, both the top and bottom numbers need to be rooted. Try looking at the bottom number again to see what multiplied by itself makes that denominator.
