## Question 17
Problem: 17. A wheelchair ramp rises 1.5 m over a horizontal run of 3.6 m. Find the length of the ramp surface. Round to two decimal places where needed.

### Student Work: 
1.5^2 + 3.6^2 = c^2
= √15.21
= 3.9

## Question 18
Problem: 18. A triangular garden has two perpendicular sides measuring 9 m and 12 m. Find the third side, then find the total length of fencing needed to surround the garden.

### Student Work: 
9^2 + 12^2
= √225
= 15\text{m}

## Question 20
Problem: 20. A two-part support is made from two right triangles. In the first triangle, the legs are 7 cm and 24 cm. Its hypotenuse becomes one leg of a second right triangle whose other leg is 60 cm. Find the final length x. Show both uses of the Pythagorean theorem.

### Student Work: 
7^2 + 24^2 = r^2
= 49 + 576 = √625
= 25\text{cm}
25^2 + 60^2
= √4225
= 65\text{cm}
